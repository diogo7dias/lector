Plus and minus for the in-book menu

Same look as 0.35.0 (UI font, black selection, hint buttons). Only the open and close control changes. Each option shows everything closed next to Navigate open, drawn at the real 480×800. Tap a screen to fire a fake e-ink refresh.

1 to 5 also fix one thing: only the five tabs open and close. POSITION, MARKS and the other groups become small labels inside the tab. Today they are sections of their own, so opening Navigate shows nothing (it owns no rows).

Pick by number, mix freely (e.g. "4, with 5's preview line"). Sizes are in device pixels.
