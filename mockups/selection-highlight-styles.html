lector — selection highlight styles

The three shipped Selection Style options, on the same Settings screen. Simulated e-ink paper, 480 px wide, real metrics (30 px rows widened to 40 px for legibility on screen).

Notes

  • Section heading bands stay a solid fill in every style. Today the heading and the selected row look identical on purpose; any non-solid selection style makes them tell apart, which is a bonus.
  • Styles that keep the text black (Brackets, Caret) survive e-ink partial refresh with less ghosting than a full row invert, and redraw faster.
  • All three are painted as plain fillRect bars from src/components/themes/SelectionStyle.h, routed through BaseTheme::drawSelection. No new GfxRenderer primitive.
