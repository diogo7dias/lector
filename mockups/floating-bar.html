The outline, now its own setting

Two independent switches. Floating Bar lifts the bar off the frame. Bar Outline draws a thin frame around the whole track so the empty part is visible too. Either one works without the other, so all four combinations are valid.

Bottom edge and two stacked bars

The rule

One small margin, used three times. The bar moves in from its outer edge by that amount, and both ends move in from the left and right sides by the same amount. Nothing else changes: same thickness setting, same top-or-bottom choice, same fill direction, same space reserved for the reading text.

Recommended value: 6 pixels (option C). Small enough to read as a detail, big enough that the gap survives the e-ink panel's edge, where a 1 or 2 pixel gap can disappear entirely.
